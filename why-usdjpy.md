## Why USD/JPY?

Most retail trading systems are built on crypto, indices, or gold —
markets that tend to produce smooth equity curves and attractive screenshots.

ZenWave is built on **USD/JPY** for a different reason.

For decades, USD/JPY has been one of the most heavily analyzed and traded currency pairs in the world.  
Institutional quantitative funds, carry desks, macro hedge funds, and central banks have continuously arbitraged away obvious inefficiencies.

What remains are **structural opportunities** tied to:
- regime changes  
- volatility expansions  
- monetary policy divergence  

These are not edges that respond well to pattern mining, curve fitting, or frequent re-optimization.  
They require patience, bounded risk, and position-level discipline.

---

## A Proving Ground, Not a Playground

USD/JPY is unforgiving to weak logic.

There are no liquidity excuses.  
No persistent “manipulation” narratives.  
No reliance on indicator lag or curve-smoothing techniques.

If a strategy survives on USD/JPY across multiple market regimes, it usually does so for **structural reasons**, not cosmetic ones.

This is why USD/JPY serves as a proving ground.

It exposes fragility early.  
It does not tolerate hidden leverage.  
It punishes systems that rely on stability rather than resilience.

---

## The Trade-Off We Chose

ZenWave could have been built differently.

It would have been possible to:
- optimize for smoother equity curves  
- prioritize faster subscriber growth  
- select instruments that produce more visually appealing results  

Instead, we chose the pair most likely to reveal structural flaws — even if that meant looking wrong for extended periods.

That choice explains why:
- equity curves are sometimes uncomfortable  
- long losing streaks are shown publicly  
- drawdowns are accepted rather than optimized away  

This project is not designed to look smooth.  
It is designed to **remain intact under stress**.

---

## Tail Events Are the Baseline

USD/JPY is shaped by policy risk.

In September 2022, the Bank of Japan intervened in FX markets for the first time in 24 years, producing a multi-percent move in minutes.  
For many retail systems, events like this are catastrophic surprises.

ZenWave engines are designed with such scenarios as **baseline assumptions**, not edge cases.

Volatility expansions, policy shocks, and abrupt regime shifts are treated as inevitable features of the market — not anomalies to be smoothed away.

---

## What This Implies for Performance

If the engines fail, they will fail:
- within predefined risk limits  
- under transparent, documented rules  
- without discretionary intervention  

If they succeed, the path will not be linear, smooth, or comfortable.

Returns are expected to come from a small number of structurally significant periods, not from constant activity or high win rates.

This approach filters for:
- patience  
- discipline  
- long-term thinking  

It is not designed for short-term excitement or visual appeal.

---

## Credibility Over Convenience

Building on USD/JPY is a constraint.  
That constraint is intentional.

It trades convenience for credibility,  
comfort for robustness,  
and appearance for structural integrity.

That is the trade-off ZenWave accepts.
